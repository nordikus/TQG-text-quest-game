## Sound effects

All recordings below are used under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), which allows commercial use and redistribution. The game uses edited excerpts and loops.

- Footsteps on leaves, soil, stone, wood and gravel by TinyWorlds, edited from public-domain recordings. [Different steps](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud)
- Fire crackle by AntumDeluge. [Fire Crackling](https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling)
- Forest birds by Magnesus. [Forest birds – ambient seamless loop](https://freesound.org/people/Magnesus/sounds/723913/)
- Spring water by TyberiusGames. [Waterflow Sound](https://opengameart.org/content/waterflow-sound)
- Doors, lock, chest and page turns by laleksic. [Various Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/various-sound-effects)
- Portcullis chain by bart. [Chain winch sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/chain-winch-sounds)
- Pickups, magic and the sound of the Great Tree rising by rubberduck. [80 CC0 RPG SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx)
- Menu click by Kenney. [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
